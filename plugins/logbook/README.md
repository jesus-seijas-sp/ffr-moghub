# MogHub Logbook

A [MogHub](../../README.md) plugin for **FINAL FANTASY RESONANCE**: your progress in one screen, with its own
main-menu entry (one of MogHub's spare entries).

## Features

- **Summary:** chapter, treasure chests opened, item spots found, tablet shards found, areas visited, side quests
  completed and in progress, Colosseum battles cleared and wins, party members, Visions and encounters.
- **Areas:** every town, dungeon and island field by region, with its treasure chests and item spots (the sparkling
  pickups): which ones you have found and what each holds (what's left can be hidden in the settings).
- **Side quests:** by region: completed, in progress with the current stage and goal, ready to take (and who gives
  it), or not available yet (names can be hidden).
- **Colosseum:** each rank battle cleared (and how many times), open (with the recommended level) or locked (with
  what unlocks it).
- **Tablet shards:** where all 99 are, area by area, and which you have picked up; shards held, and what the collector
  gives for each number handed over.

Everything is in the game's own words, in all its languages: names, goals and places come from the game's string
tables. The Logbook only reads your game, through the game's own functions and conditions; it changes nothing.

## Install

Download it from [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/finalfantasyresonance/mods/112). Install
[MogHub](https://www.nexusmods.com/finalfantasyresonance/mods/110), then extract the Logbook into the game folder: it adds
`FFRS\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\MogHubLogbook`.

## Development

- `runtime/MogHubLogbook/moghub.lua`: settings and the menu entry; `Scripts/progress.lua`: the live reads;
  `Scripts/view.lua`: the screen.
- `data/logbook_data.lua` (IDs, conditions and string keys) is generated by `tools/export_data.py` from the game's
  tables and the chests and item spots placed in the levels (see the tool's header for the dump commands), and the
  Bestiary's area grouping.
